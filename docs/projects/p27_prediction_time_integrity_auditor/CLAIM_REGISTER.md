# Claim Register Contract

Every public claim must be represented in the machine-readable claim register.

Allowed classifications:

- MEASURED
- DERIVED
- CONTROLLED_INJECTION
- PROJECT_DEFINED_METRIC
- INTERPRETATION
- LIMITATION
- FUTURE_WORK
- PREVIEW_ONLY
- UNSUPPORTED

Final dashboard, video, and manuscript claims require evidence paths and
validation status.

PREVIEW_ONLY and UNSUPPORTED claims are prohibited from final public release.
